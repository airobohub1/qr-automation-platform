Frequently Asked Questions

What is AI ROBO HUB QR Automation Platform? ▼
It is a smart QR generation and automation platform to create Single & Bulk QR codes with usage limits, business tracking, dashboard analytics, and subscription control.
What are the available plans? ▼
We offer Free, Paid, and Enterprise plans. Each plan has daily and total QR generation limits.
What happens if my Free plan QR limit is over? ▼
You will be restricted from generating further QR codes and prompted to upgrade your plan. Our sales executive will contact you after submitting the upgrade form.
How do I upgrade my plan? ▼
Click on Paid or Enterprise plan → Submit Lead Form → Our team will reach out to complete your upgrade.
Why do I need to activate my account? ▼
Activation verifies your email and secures your account. Without activation you cannot login.
My activation link expired. What should I do? ▼
Go to Login page → Click Resend Activation → You will receive a fresh link.
Why am I getting “Please Login to Continue” error? ▼
Your session has expired or cookies are blocked. Please login again to refresh your session.
How long does my login session remain active? ▼
Sessions are valid for 24 hours. After expiry you must login again.
Is my data safe and secure? ▼
Yes. We use signed session tokens, password hashing, email verification, and secure cookies.
Can I generate QR codes in bulk? ▼
Yes. Bulk QR generation is available via Excel upload from your dashboard.
How do I contact support? ▼
You can reach our support team via the contact details provided in your activation email.
